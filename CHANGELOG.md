# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

First version, for Foundry VTT v14 and the PF2e system 8.x.

- **GM screen window** (GM only): opens from the Token controls, with Shift+G, or from a macro. It has Rules, Party and Treasure tabs; cards you can collapse and pin; a search box; and it remembers its size, position and active tab.
- **Rules tab:**
  - DCs by level, simple DCs and adjustments, and degrees of success
  - conditions and actions from the PF2e compendiums: click to read, drag to apply
  - dying, wounded and hero points; cover, terrain, light and falling; Recall Knowledge
  - links into the PF2e GM Screen journal
  - a GM notes card
- **Party tab:**
  - a live row per PC: HP (with +/- buttons), AC, saves, Perception, class/spell DC, hero points, conditions, speeds and senses
  - drop conditions or effects onto a row to apply them
  - open a PC's sheet or ping their token from the row
  - the party's lowest defenses at a glance
  - blind group checks
  - choose which characters are shown
- **Treasure tab:**
  - GM Core party treasure by level
  - a budget tracker adjusted for party size
  - a loot roller that draws from the PF2e equipment compendium, with post to chat and add to Party stash
  - a coin splitter
